# Foundations and device experiments

These eleven lesson IDs now each open a distinct interactive native experiment through `FoundationsLessonLab(lessonID:)`. Broad lesson titles are introduced through named finite models. Their implementation does not imply a universal symbolic solver, complete semiconductor device library, or equivalence to every university laboratory.

Every experiment provides bounded controls, numerical readings, quantitatively labeled plots, a governing equation, model assumptions and a prediction with a checkable explanation. Compared curves use one shared printed physical scale. Prefixes such as mA, mC, kΩ and µF are explicit; the pure models accept SI values. Invalid or undefined configurations produce nil and a visible unavailable-result message, including the exact RC pole.

## Implemented experiments

| Lesson ID | Experiment and controls | Prediction/check | Model boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| `calculus` | Sine-plus-DC and ramp current; current −2…2 mA, amplitude 0…2 mA, frequency 0.1…5 Hz, ramp slope −1…1 mA/s, inspected time 0…3 s. Separate current and transferred-charge plots. | Full-period sine area cancels; a zero-initial-current ramp's transferred charge scales with time squared. | Imposed analytic waveform, Q(0)=0; no arbitrary drawn waveform, symbolic integration or microscopic charge transport. |
| `linear-algebra` | A fixed two-node conductance matrix: change source 0…12 V, R2/R3 0.2…10 kΩ; R1=1 kΩ and R4=2 kΩ. Inspect matrix, node voltages and KCL. A second mode changes RC frequency 1…2000 Hz and C 0.1…10 µF, showing real/imaginary H on the same scale. | Linear node voltages scale with supply; a transfer ratio and phase do not scale with signal amplitude. | Grounded positive-resistance topology and unloaded RC phasor. No arbitrary current injections, singular-topology editor or general linear-algebra engine. |
| `differential-equations` | Initial/final voltages 0…2 V, τ 0.1…3 s, h/τ 0.05…2.5. Compare exact, forward-Euler and backward-Euler samples at the same times. | Forward error multiplier 1−h/τ grows in magnitude beyond h=2τ; physical stability and numerical stability differ. | Scalar constant-input linear ODE. No nonlinear or adaptive ODE engine. |
| `probability` | Exact transformed-current CDF for a uniform resistor tolerance. V 1…12 V, nominal R 0.5…5 kΩ, width 0…30%, threshold 0…30 mA. Add 5…500 repeatable pseudorandom measurements, seed 1…99 and fixed calibration bias −1…1 mA. Inspect mean, sample s, standard error and running mean. | Mean V/R exceeds the nominal current for nonzero symmetric width; averaging cannot remove a fixed offset. | The uniform law and independence are assumptions, not inferred manufacturer statistics. Seeded SplitMix64 draws, no correlated data or extra instrument noise; s/√N is an estimate, not a guaranteed confidence interval. |
| `matter` | Four modes: carrier conductivity (log density 16…26 m⁻³, mobility 0.001…0.2 m²/Vs, ±2 V); adiabatic heat (1…20 W, 20…200 g, 400…4200 J/kgK, 0…60 s); photons (200…1000 nm); mechanical work (0…20 N, 0…3 m, 0…180°). Each has a physical quantitative plot. | Density doubles conductivity; mass doubles heat capacity; wavelength doubles to halve photon energy; a perpendicular force does zero work. | One low-field uniform carrier species; fixed heat capacity with no cooling/phase change; one free-space photon; constant imposed force and straight displacement. No band, chemistry, quantum many-body or spatial thermal solver. |
| `inductors` | Energize/release a closed ideal R–L loop. V 1…12 V, R 10…1000 Ω, L 1…500 mH, time 0…5τ. Inspect current, voltage, energy and slope. | Release preserves initial current; its starting inductor voltage is −RI₀. | Release shorts the independent source and keeps a resistive current path. No opened-switch arc, core saturation, parasitic capacitance or winding losses beyond R. |
| `resonance` | Series RLC step and AC-drive modes. R 1…200 Ω, L 1…100 mH, C 1…100 µF, source 1…10 V. Compare damping regimes and capacitor step voltage; sweep current amplitude, selected phase, Q, half-power bandwidth and capacitor peak over 0.1…3f₀. | Rcritical=2√(L/C); current resonance is at f₀ and Ipeak=Vpeak/R. BW=R/(2πL); at both half-power frequencies Ipeak=Ires/√2. | Zero-state step or sinusoidal steady state; ideal lumped positive elements. No distributed resonance, nonlinearities, ratings or frequency-dependent losses. |
| `network-analysis` | Loaded divider/Thevenin equivalence with V 1…12 V, top/bottom R 0.2…5 kΩ, load 0.1…10 kΩ. Compare original/equivalent voltages and load power. Laplace mode changes RC and complex coordinate s; shows H(s), pole and impulse response. | Resistive load power peaks at RL=Rth; this is 50% equivalent-source efficiency. Doubling RC halves pole magnitude but preserves impulse area. | One independent-source divider and one zero-state RC transfer, not arbitrary dependent-source/mesh/two-port/symbolic-transform analysis. Outside Re(s)>−1/RC, displayed H is an algebraic continuation rather than a convergent Laplace integral. |
| `bjt` | Reciprocal two-junction Ebers–Moll. VBE −0.2…0.78 V, VCE 0…2 V, βF 20…300, T 250…350 K; fixed transport IS=1 fA, αR=0.5. Inspect three signed terminal currents, KCL, region and derivative. | VBC=VBE−VCE becomes positive as VCE falls below VBE; forward-active current-gain reasoning then fails. | Low injection, imposed terminal voltages, reciprocal IS parameters. IS stays fixed when T changes. No Early effect, high injection, breakdown, series resistance, self-heating or charge-storage timing. |
| `mosfet` | Long-channel NMOS output curves with VGS/VDS 0…5 V, threshold 0.5…2 V and β=μCoxW/L 0.1…3 mA/V². Inspect cutoff/triode/saturation, ID and gm. | Doubling overdrive gives fourfold saturation current and twice gm, provided VDS still exceeds overdrive. | λ=0, body tied to source; no subthreshold, body effect, short-channel effects, capacitance, thermal changes or breakdown. |
| `analog-design` | Quasistatic common-emitter load-line solver. Forward-active target current 0.05…2 mA, RC 0.5…5 kΩ, VCC 5…12 V, input 0.1…25 mV peak and inspected phase. Compare nonlinear output with its local tangent on the same voltage scale; compare analytic gain with a ±1 µV derivative. | Smaller perturbations approach the tangent; actual bias headroom determines whether the requested active current is realizable. | Reciprocal Ebers–Moll, ideal imposed base voltage, fixed IS/βF/αR/T; bisection operating point. No Early effect, input loading, noise, capacitance or bandwidth. Phase is a plotting coordinate, not a frequency-response claim. |

## Governing-model references

Primary references were checked on October 11, 2026. The application-specific topology, transformed-resistor distribution and load-line calculations are derived from these governing relations rather than copied laboratory exercises.

- Calculus: [MIT Calculus, fundamental theorem and applications](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-18-001-calculus-fall-2023/pages/open-textbook/). Current is the derivative of transferred charge; the displayed sine/ramp antiderivatives are evaluated analytically.
- Nodal matrices: [SpiceSharp's own modified-nodal-analysis derivation](https://spicesharp.github.io/SpiceSharp/articles/tutorials/writing_behaviors/modified_nodal_analysis.html). The lesson's fixed two-node matrix follows KCL with g=1/R.
- ODE methods: [MIT forward and backward Euler notes](https://web.mit.edu/10.001/Web/Course_Notes/Differential_Equations_Notes/node3.html). The h<2τ criterion follows their linear decay stability condition.
- Probability: [MIT continuous expectation/variance reading](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/resources/mit18_05_s22_class06-prep-a_pdf/). For R~Uniform(a,b), integrating V/R and V²/R² gives the current moments; the resistance-to-current mapping reverses interval order.
- Matter: [MIT drift-current/conductivity lecture](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-012-microelectronic-devices-and-circuits-spring-2009/4692cf463514c4f47d8629fab7d41c15_MIT6_012S09_lec03.pdf), [OpenStax heat capacity](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/1-4-heat-transfer-specific-heat-and-calorimetry), [OpenStax photon energy](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/6-2-photoelectric-effect) and [OpenStax constant-force work](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/7-1-work). Their relations connect conductivity, accumulated energy and work projection.
- Inductance: [OpenStax RL circuits](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/14-5-rl-circuits). The lab solves L di/dt+Ri=Vs with stated initial current and a closed release loop.
- RLC: [MIT damping and impedance materials](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-03sc-differential-equations-fall-2011/pages/unit-ii-second-order-constant-coefficient-linear-equations/applications-lrc-circuits-damping-ratio/) and [MIT series resonance and bandwidth lecture](https://web.mit.edu/6.101/www/s2019/handouts/L02_4.pdf). The capacitor-voltage step is solved across under/critical/overdamped regimes; the AC current uses the series impedance and exact current half-power bandwidth.
- Network equivalents/transforms: [MIT Thévenin/Norton lecture](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/resources/6002_l3/) and [MIT circuit/Laplace modeling notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003-modeling-dynamics-and-control-i-spring-2005/12904eb02b9e301f891c12c7b26226be_archive.pdf). The RC impulse response is the inverse of 1/(1+sRC), not an implemented arbitrary inverse-transform engine.
- BJT and analog: [Analog Devices transistor models](https://wiki.analog.com/university/courses/electronics/text/chapter-8) and [single-transistor amplifier stages](https://wiki.analog.com/university/courses/electronics/text/chapter-9). The reciprocal two-junction currents enforce terminal KCL; differentiating the collector-load equation gives Av=−gmRC/(1+goRC).
- MOSFET: [MIT MOSFET large-signal regions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-012-microelectronic-devices-and-circuits-spring-2009/f539d85c15f389afd2290abfc6ca4318_MIT6_012S09_lec10.pdf). The lesson explicitly sets channel-length modulation to zero and uses the same β convention in both region formulas.

## Verification boundaries

`Tests/AdvancedModelsTests/FoundationsModelsTests.swift` contains independent analytic, conservation, derivative, quadrature, distribution, boundary and invalid-input checks for these eleven IDs. They include numerical integration of charge/current moments, independent series/parallel nodal reduction, KCL and energy derivatives, all three RLC damping regimes, Thévenin terminal equivalence, transistor-region continuity, and a finite-difference check of the common-emitter derivative.

The original document reported that the pure Foundation model file typechecked under Swift 6 and that the native labs typechecked against the iOS 27 Simulator SDK with the shared lesson components. Those are historical reports; corresponding compiler output is not included in the public snapshot. See the [public validation guide](Validation.md) for available tests, runnable commands and the evidence status of integrated tests, native interactions and physical-device install/launch. These outcomes were not rerun during the documentation repair.
